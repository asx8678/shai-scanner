- **Level 2 (Moderate):** 1-hour response, project lead
- **Level 3 (Critical):** 15-minute response, all hands
- **Level 4 (Emergency):** Immediate response, all available team

### Emergency Contacts
- **Security Vulnerability:** security@shai-scanner.dev
- **Legal Threat:** legal@shai-scanner.dev
- **PR Crisis:** pr-crisis@shai-scanner.dev
- **Technical Issues:** tech-lead@shai-scanner.dev
