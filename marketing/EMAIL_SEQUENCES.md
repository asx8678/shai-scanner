## ⚙️ Automation Workflow

### Trigger Conditions
- **Pre-Launch:** User subscribes before launch date
- **Launch Day:** Launch date/time + active user
- **Post-Launch:** 3 days after installation
- **Re-engagement:** No activity for 14 days

### Wait Times
**Pre-Launch:** 2 days between emails
**Launch Day:** 2-3 hours between emails
**Post-Launch:** 4-7 days between emails
**Re-engagement:** 7 days between emails

### A/B Testing
**Test:** Subject lines, content length, CTAs, timing
**Process:** 10% sample → 4-hour test → winner to 80%

### Unsubscribe Handling
**Options:** Full unsubscribe, marketing only, reduce frequency, pause
**Compliance:** CAN-SPAM, GDPR, one-click unsubscribe
