## 🎯 Quick Start

### For Immediate Use:
1. **Choose your stakeholder type**: technical, business, security, or community
2. **Copy the appropriate example** from `STAKEHOLDER_UPDATE_EXAMPLES.md`
3. **Personalize** with recipient name, organization, and specific metrics
4. **Send** and track engagement

### For Customization:
1. **Read the guide** in `STAKEHOLDER_UPDATE_GUIDE.md`
2. **Select sections** from `STAKEHOLDER_UPDATE.md` based on stakeholder needs
3. **Customize** with organization-specific details
4. **Test** with sample recipients before full send

## 🛠️ Customization Tips

### Personalization Variables:
- `[Stakeholder Name]` - Recipient's full name
- `[Stakeholder Title]` - Recipient's job title
- `[Organization Name]` - Recipient's organization
- `[Primary Metric]` - Most relevant metric for this stakeholder
- `[Call-to-Action URL]` - Specific CTA link

### Industry Adjustments:
- **Technology**: Emphasize performance and integration
- **Financial Services**: Focus on compliance and risk reduction
- **Healthcare**: Highlight HIPAA and data security
- **Government**: Stress security clearances and air-gapped support

### Tone Adjustments:
- **Formal**: For executive communications
- **Semi-Formal**: For professional stakeholder updates
- **Friendly**: For community and open source communications
