## 📊 Template Best Practices

### Subject Lines

**Effective Patterns:**
- "Quick question about [Topic]"
- "Save [Money] on [Topic]"
- "Free [Resource] for [Audience]"
- "[Mutual Connection] suggested I reach out"

**Avoid:**
- ALL CAPS
- Excessive punctuation!!!
- Spam trigger words (free, guarantee, etc.)
- Misleading content

### Opening Lines

**Effective Approaches:**
- Reference mutual connection
- Mention specific company/initiative
- Reference recent news or achievement
- Ask a relevant question

**Avoid:**
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "I'm writing to introduce..."
- "As per my previous email..."

### Value Proposition

**Structure:**
1. **Problem:** What challenge do they face?
2. **Solution:** How does Shai-Scanner help?
3. **Proof:** What results have others seen?
4. **Next Step:** What should they do?

### Call-to-Action

**Effective CTAs:**
- "Would you be open to a 15-minute call?"
- "Can I send you more information?"
- "Would you like to try it free?"
- "Should we schedule a demo?"

**Avoid:**
- Multiple CTAs in one email
- Vague requests ("let me know")
- High-commitment requests too early

### Follow-up Cadence

**Recommended Sequence:**
1. **Day 0:** Initial outreach
2. **Day 3:** Follow-up if no response
3. **Day 7:** Second follow-up with value add
4. **Day 14:** Final follow-up
5. **Day 30:** Re-engagement attempt

**Timing:**
- Tuesday-Thursday: Best response rates
- 10 AM or 2 PM: Optimal send times
- Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons

- Target: 10%+
- Measurement: Per email

- Target: 5%+
- Measurement: Per campaign

- Target: 2%+
- Measurement: Per campaign
- Optimization: Follow-up sequence

### A/B Testing

**Test Elements:**
- Subject lines
- Opening lines
- Value propositions
- CTAs
- Send times

**Testing Process:**
1. Create two variants
2. Send to small sample
3. Measure results
4. Use winner for full send
5. Repeat
